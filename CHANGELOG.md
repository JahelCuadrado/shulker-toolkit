# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-24

- **Middle-click windows at the work stations:** open your shulker boxes, bundles and ender chest
  next to the stonecutter, loom, cartography table, smithing table, grindstone and anvil too.
- **Windows that stay with you:** they stay open when you go straight from one of these screens to
  another, for example between work stations with Handy Tables.
- **Room on any screen:** on small screens or with the recipe book open, windows shrink to fit.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-18

First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Everything you need to live out of shulker boxes: see
inside them, move items in and out without placing them, craft, build and refill straight from
them.

- See inside shulker boxes from the tooltip, in a chest-style window with the box's colour and the
  selected item's name; your ender chest over an End starfield and, optionally, bundles.
- Move items in and out without placing the box: right click (or split like bundles), drag across
  slots, one item at a time with `Ctrl`/`Cmd` and the mouse wheel. Works with your ender chest too.
- Look inside boxes and bundles nested in other boxes.
- Icon markers: a fill bar and a green + when the box already holds the item you carry and has room.
- Middle-click windows: open up to 6 boxes, bundles or your ender chest next to your inventory,
  crafting table, chest or furnace and use them like chests.
- Craft from your boxes with the recipe book, with four depth levels up to the bundles inside the
  boxes in your ender chest.
- Pick block takes the block out of your shulker boxes (one block or a full stack).
- Auto refill of blocks, food and potions from your shulker boxes.
- Optional pickup into boxes: when your inventory is full, or straight into the box that holds the
  item.
- Litematica Easy Place, manual placement and the Litematica Printer take blocks from your boxes.
- Simple settings screen with no extra libraries (Mod Menu supported).
- Works on servers without the mod (tooltips and icon markers only); server owners can turn
  features off.
- Available in English, Español (España), Español (México), Português (Brasil), Português
  (Portugal), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, Українська,
  简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
